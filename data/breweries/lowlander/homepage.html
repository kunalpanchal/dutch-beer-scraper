The Marlborough Head

Covent Garden

A lively pub & kitchen tucked away on historic Drury Lane in the heart of London’s West End. Pouring classic lagers, craft beers, and a curated selection of wines and premium spirits, all served alongside a delicious, seasonal food menu.

Elevated pub grub in the heart of Covent Garden

From leisurely lunches and pre-theatre bites to relaxed dinners, The Marlborough Head serves up seasonal British cooking with a modern twist. Expect comforting pub classics, lighter plates and bold flavours, all freshly prepared by our kitchen team. On Sundays, settle in for one of our traditional roasts with all the trimmings. Whatever the occasion, there's always something worth tucking into on Drury Lane.

Private hire at The Marlborough Head

Looking for the ideal spot to host your next event? The Marlborough Head offers a stunning blend of classic charm and modern style. Our venue can accommodate groups ranging from as few as 10 to as many as 120 guests. Whether you’re organising a birthday, engagement, corporate meeting, or simply a casual get-together with family and friends, The Marlborough Head is the perfect choice.
